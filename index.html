Good to know

Questions,
answered.

How do I book?

Send the booking form below or DM us on Instagram @artt_nailsss. We'll reply to confirm your time.

Can I bring my own inspo?

Absolutely — send screenshots with your booking. We love recreating ideas and adding our own artistic twist.

How long does a set last?

Gel and BIAB usually last around 3–4 weeks with good aftercare. We'll share tips to keep them looking fresh.

How long is an appointment?

A simple gel set is quicker; extensions and detailed art take longer. We'll give you a time estimate when we confirm.

What if a nail breaks?

Message us — small repairs can be booked between appointments.
